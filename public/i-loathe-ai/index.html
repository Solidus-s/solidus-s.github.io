I loathe AI

October 29, 2026

My views on AI

In my personal opinion I believe AI is pretty overhyped and is something that shouldn’t be used to the extent it is used nowadays.

Of course there are plenty of better articles that give reasons why AI is bad, but for my sanity I won’t go that deep on those things. I will give a couple of links in case you are interested.

And me personally I never want to touch any LLM in my life ever again, I used it allot when it started out fresh. But began to despise it the more and more I used it.

Overhyped

I find it strange that so many of my friends and family are so interested in AI in the first place. In my eyes there is nothing special about AI.

They tell me “Use it! Everyone uses it.” directly or indirectly.

Do we really need to follow this hype train? Seriously? Its like people are afraid of getting left behind by not using this technology, but they should be happy that they are not using this.

“Benefits”

There are so many benefits to not using AI, and very little to gain from using AI. Cause lets be honest… What benefits is a normal person getting compared to a AI monolith company? Even if you aren’t paying a single cent to these AI companies, I hope people are aware of the fact that their chats are 100% recorded.

“But what about for productivity?”- Who cares? It doesn’t really matter to be honest, productivity is just a measurement used by idiots.

Dehumanization

AI is used to take everything great about humanity and puts something that isn’t worth being amazed at.

It takes the soul out of people’s eyes when it used in pictures. It takes the craft of art and replaces it with something that has no emotion.

I just find it sad and frustrating that people use these AI models for pictures and art that they can very much do themselves, despite how much they secretly doubt themselves.

I would say bad and messy art has more merit than whatever concoction AI generated images are.

“Look I made this xyz thing using AI”

No you didn’t. You didn’t make it.

My notes

I actually made a draft similar to article but when I first took a second to read and understand what I typed out… It was very emotional and argumentative to say the very least.

It frustrates me how people are still mentally in the AI bubble, especially those close to me.

Maybe it will change in the future. But who knows :)

In my opinion its probably better to live your life without AI.
