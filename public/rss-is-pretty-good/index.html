RSS is pretty good

November 1, 2026

What is RSS?

RSS stands for Really Simple Syndication. its main use is for getting updates from websites that use RSS in one nice app. For example: blogs (like mine), news, youtube, etc,.

Where to find RSS

Most blogs will have an RSS link on their page. If you copy the link on the RSS button on my website and paste it into your favorite feed app, you now have a nice way to get updates on my website without having to search for it on your browser.

Sometimes it isn’t really that easy and you will have to look a little bit. Fret not, I have two links to save the day.

One is a youtube RSS link, if you take the link and put your favorite youtuber’s channel ID at the end of the link, you now have a feed. The way to find your youtuber’s channel ID is to go on their page, click “more” right above the subscribe button, share channel and then copy channel ID. Every youtuber has a unique channel ID that is a bunch of random letters and numbers.

Two is a RSS finder website, you put the website’s link you want a RSS for and it finds it for you. In some cases, websites will just straight up not have a feed for you to grab.

What app to use?

Honestly it is up to you. I can only recommend two in my opinion. One is NetNewsWire which is really nice to use on MacOS and iOS. Two is Mutt if you want RSS feeds in the terminal, it does take some setup though.

Why bother?

Nowadays people are spoon-fed content and endlessly consume things they really hate or will inevitably forget. With RSS you most likely will still consume endlessly, but at least you have the choice of what you want to see.

It is also a nice way to avoid the news – lets be honest humans have a negativity bias, and news outlets abuse that constantly. You can also be fed propaganda on news sites.

With youtube, well youtube is designed to be very addictive and hook users in.

Stop consuming slop and things you hate. It is important that we are intentional with what we expose ourselves in a day and age of endless content and information.
