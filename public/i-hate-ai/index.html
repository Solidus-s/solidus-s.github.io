My ethical concerns

Everyone knows AI uses allot of fresh water (you know… the thing humans need to survive) and electricity. AI is heavily unregulated.

AI is used to make slop and propaganda. It hasn’t been so much easier.

AI in Linux

Linus Torvalds the creator and head maintainer of Linux Kernel is fine with using AI for writing code, even though its against the very spirit of the GPL. What is funny is that Linus wants Linux to be used by people, because what would be the point of it existing if there isn’t a community? Well how will some people afford computers with the infamous rampocalypse and rising GPU and SSD prices to even use linux if you’re using the very thing that is causing all this. Maybe I am splitting hairs here but it seems hypocritical to me.

Actually there is a story about AI code and the GPL, look at the GZDoom drama.

“Use it! Everyone uses it!”

Why? Should I start doing drugs and alcohol too because everyone uses it or does it? I really don’t like this argument, just because everyone does something doesn’t mean its good.

AI images should NOT be considered as art

I am frankly disgusted with AI “art”. It isn’t even art to me, it’s just a generated image.

Art is meant for humans made by humans.

These AI generated pictures have no soul, no discipline, nothing of value.

What is humourous is how people will say it is good for creativity… why does it all look the same then? Have they even seen real art?

I really don’t understand how these people exist. They must really hate the joy of being creative. Going at it for a long time, you try that one crazy idea and it works out, looking beautiful to you for a few minutes… then never seeing it again and repeating the cycle.

What is AI good for then?

In my opinion I think it is only good for niche cases such as medical uses, where AI is used for early detection of certain diseases based on genes.

Again LLMs are not the only kind of AI, even with that in mind there is just simply not that many good use cases. I can only think of bad cases for example, Being to used to bomb people, surveil and invade the personal privacy of internet users, propaganda, etc,.

AI isn’t magic

If you’re a big AI advocate do you even understand how this technology works? AI doesn’t understand what it is outputting, sure it looks like it is but it can’t understand the way we humans do.

The way AI works is that it has a hidden layer i.e. processing layer that has a bunch of nodes with tuned values. The AI outputs something based on what information it is given (trained on) and what value these nodes are. In LLMs words have special connections, like how a autocorrector works.

AI doesn’t have the deep knowledge and appreciation for the things it has generated unlike humans.

Remember! AI is good at pretending, its just a machine at the end of the day.

The breaking point

AI doesn’t have emotions. Sure neural networks are just an emulation of how the brain works, but it is not the same.

It is kinda like memories and memorabilia.

Memories aren’t just sounds and pictures. They exist somewhere between the sounds, between the pictures.

When a soldier pulls a trigger on someone, he has to live with the fact he has done that for the rest of his life. AI doesn’t have emotions, it will happily follow instructions and bomb someone without a second thought. Then who is held respondsible? No one, because AI has no ethics, no empathy, no rhyme or reason to exist other than to kill.

AI has to be seriously questioned as it is affecting what makes us human. But these AI companies want everyone to use AI, to not question what is wrong with it, so that legislation isn’t catching up on time.
